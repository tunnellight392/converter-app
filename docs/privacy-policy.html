Privacy Policy

Unit Converter · Last updated: 19 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Unit Converter mobile application (the “App”) handles your information. Your privacy is important to us, and we have built the App to work entirely on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App performs all unit conversions locally on your device. It does not require an account, and it does not request internet access.

  • No personal data is collected.
  • No usage analytics or tracking is performed.
  • No advertising identifiers are used.
  • No data is shared with third parties.

Permissions

The App does not request any sensitive permissions and has no access to the internet, your contacts, location, camera, files, or other personal data.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from within the App, the text you type is passed to your own email application so you can send it to us. The App itself does not store or transmit your message — sending the email is handled entirely by the email app you select, through your email provider. If you contact us this way, we will see the email address and message you send, and use them only to respond to your feedback.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
